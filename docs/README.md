# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme's project management guidance, from validating a new initiative through delivery, release, and continuous improvement. Use the overview for a concise introduction, then follow the relevant guide for detailed practices and checklists.

## Project management process

OctoAcme initiates projects by validating the business need and desired outcomes, identifying stakeholders, and confirming readiness to proceed. The team then plans scope, a prioritized backlog, milestones, risks, dependencies, and ownership. During execution, work is delivered iteratively with regular team and stakeholder communication, quality checks, and escalation of blockers. Releases use pre-release checks, deployment verification, and rollback readiness. After a sprint, release, milestone, or incident, the team captures lessons in a retrospective and tracks improvement actions.

Core roles include the Project Manager (PM), who coordinates delivery; the Product Manager, who defines outcomes and priorities; developers, who build and test; QA/testing, who validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders, who provide input and approvals. See the [Roles & Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) guide for details.
